# ── STEP 3: DAX Measures ────────────────────────────────────
# Create these in fact_sales_forecast table
# Home → New Measure → paste formula → Enter

# 3.1 Forecasted Demand Tomorrow
Forecasted Demand Tomorrow =
CALCULATE(
    SUM(fact_sales_forecast[forecasted_quantity]),
    fact_sales_forecast[forecast_date] = TODAY() + 1
)

# 3.2 Forecast Accuracy %
# Compares yesterday's forecast to actual sales — builds trust in the model
Forecast Accuracy % =
VAR actual =
    CALCULATE(
        SUM(fact_sales[quantity]),
        fact_sales[date] = TODAY() - 1
    )
VAR forecasted =
    CALCULATE(
        SUM(fact_sales_forecast[forecasted_quantity]),
        fact_sales_forecast[forecast_date] = TODAY() - 1
    )
RETURN
    IF(
        actual = 0, BLANK(),
        (1 - DIVIDE(ABS(actual - forecasted), actual)) * 100
    )

# 3.3 High Demand Hours Tomorrow
# Count of hours where forecast is close to or above upper bound
High Demand Hours Tomorrow =
CALCULATE(
    COUNTROWS(fact_sales_forecast),
    fact_sales_forecast[forecast_date] = TODAY() + 1,
    fact_sales_forecast[forecasted_quantity]
        > fact_sales_forecast[upper_bound] * 0.8
)

# 3.4 Demand Gap
# How much headroom exists between forecast and upper confidence bound
Demand Gap =
SUM(fact_sales_forecast[upper_bound])
- SUM(fact_sales_forecast[forecasted_quantity])

# 3.5 Last Refreshed (place top-right — same as anomaly page)
Last Refreshed =
"Data current as of: " & FORMAT(NOW(), "DD MMM YYYY HH:MM")

# Add a new page to your report. Name it "Demand Forecast".
# This page answers: what will happen tomorrow?

# --- KPI Cards (top row — 4 cards) ---
Card 1:  Forecasted Demand Tomorrow
Card 2:  Forecast Accuracy %          ← builds confidence in the model
Card 3:  High Demand Hours Tomorrow
Card 4:  fact_sales_forecast[training_rows_used]  ← shows model is data-rich

# --- Slicers ---
Slicer 1: dim_product[product_name]
Slicer 2: dim_store[store_name]
Slicer 3: fact_sales_forecast[forecast_date]

# --- Forecast Line Chart (main visual — full width) ---
Visual type: Line chart
X-axis: fact_sales_forecast[forecast_hour]
Lines:
  SUM(fact_sales_forecast[forecasted_quantity])  → Forecast (solid blue)
  SUM(fact_sales_forecast[upper_bound])          → Upper Bound (dashed)
  SUM(fact_sales_forecast[lower_bound])          → Lower Bound (dashed)

What to look for:
  Peak hours where the forecast line rises sharply
  → pre-position stock and staff before that hour
  Wide gap between upper and lower bound
  → model is less certain, monitor closely

# --- Top 10 Products by Forecasted Demand Tomorrow ---
Visual type: Horizontal Bar chart
Y-axis: dim_product[product_name]
X-axis: SUM(fact_sales_forecast[forecasted_quantity])
Filter: forecast_date = TODAY() + 1
Sort: descending by forecasted_quantity
Purpose: Which products need the most stock ready tomorrow morning?

Filter: date / forecast_date = TODAY() - 1
Purpose: Side by side comparison of forecast vs what actually happened.


# ── STEP 6: Demand Heatmap by Hour and Department ───────────
# Most impressive visual on this page — shows at a glance
# which department peaks at which hour tomorrow.

Visual type: Matrix
Rows:    dim_product[category]
Columns: fact_sales_forecast[forecast_hour]
Values:  SUM(fact_sales_forecast[forecasted_quantity])

Conditional formatting on values:
  Format → Conditional formatting → Background color → Gradient
  Minimum: white (low demand)
  Maximum: dark blue (high demand)

Example output:
  Category      | 8AM | 9AM | 10AM | 11AM | 12PM | 1PM |
  Toys          |  5  |  8  |  15  |  35  |  28  |  12 |
  Appliances    |  3  |  6  |  12  |  18  |  22  |  20 |
  Confectionery |  2  |  4  |   8  |  12  |  30  |  35 |

Use this to:
  Plan staffing levels by department per hour
  Schedule stock replenishment before peak hours
  Identify slow hours for promotions and offers
